# Rental Agreement Basics in India

topic: rental agreement

Before signing a rent agreement, users should review the parties' names, property address, rent amount, security deposit, lock-in period, notice period, maintenance responsibility, utility payments, repair obligations, restrictions, renewal terms, and refund conditions.

For disputes about security deposit or eviction, useful documents include the rent agreement, payment proofs, bank transfers, receipts, WhatsApp messages, emails, photographs of property condition, move-in and move-out records, inspection notes, and written notices.

Users should prefer written communication and maintain a clear timeline of rent payments, complaints, repairs, notices, and handover. For large deposits, threats, illegal lockout, police involvement, or urgent possession issues, users should consult a qualified advocate.

Rental rules can vary by state and local law. Legal Saathi can help organize facts and documents but cannot provide final legal advice.
