# FIR Basics in India

topic: FIR and police process

An FIR is generally connected with information about a cognizable offence given to police. For serious criminal matters, users should speak with a qualified advocate immediately.

A factual police complaint or FIR-related preparation note should include the date, time, place, persons involved, what happened, witnesses if any, injuries or losses, relevant documents, screenshots, photographs, CCTV details if known, and any urgent safety concern.

Users should keep copies of complaints, acknowledgements, medical records, photographs, messages, call logs, and other relevant proof. They should provide truthful information only.

Legal Saathi must not help users create false allegations, hide involvement, destroy evidence, influence witnesses illegally, evade investigation, or mislead police or courts. For arrest, bail, investigation notices, or court summons, users should consult an enrolled advocate urgently.
