# Legal Saathi General Disclaimer

topic: disclaimer

Legal Saathi provides general legal information and preparation support for users in India. It is not a law firm, advocate, or substitute for advice from a qualified advocate.

Legal procedures, limitation periods, court requirements, government portals, and local police practices can change. Users should verify current rules with official sources or a qualified advocate before acting.

For urgent matters involving arrest, bail, violence, threats, child custody, domestic violence, criminal allegations, court deadlines, or police action, users should contact a qualified advocate immediately.

Users should preserve truthful documents, messages, notices, receipts, photographs, and timelines. They should not fabricate facts, hide evidence, destroy documents, intimidate witnesses, or mislead police, courts, or advocates.
