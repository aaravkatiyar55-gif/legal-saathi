# Workplace Rights Basics in India

topic: workplace rights

Workplace disputes can involve unpaid salary, wrongful termination, harassment, discrimination, unsafe conditions, bond disputes, non-compete issues, experience letter refusal, or final settlement delay.

Useful documents include offer letter, appointment letter, employment contract, salary slips, bank statements, attendance records, emails, chat messages, HR policies, resignation or termination letters, performance records, and settlement calculations.

Users should write a clear timeline covering joining date, role, salary, key events, complaints made, employer response, and relief wanted. Written communication should remain polite and factual.

Rules may differ based on employment type, state, industry, and contract terms. For harassment, threats, large salary dues, termination, or legal notices, users should consult a qualified advocate or appropriate authority.
