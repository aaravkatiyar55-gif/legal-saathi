# Cyber Complaint Basics in India

topic: cyber complaint

Cyber complaints in India can relate to online fraud, UPI or card fraud, social media harassment, account hacking, fake profiles, phishing, identity misuse, or online threats. Users can consider reporting cyber incidents through the National Cyber Crime Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or by contacting the local police/cyber cell.

Useful preparation details include the date and time of the incident, platform or app name, phone numbers or usernames involved, transaction IDs, screenshots, URLs, email headers if available, bank or UPI references, and a short chronological summary.

For financial fraud, users should contact the bank or payment provider quickly and preserve transaction proof. They should not delete chats, screenshots, SMS messages, emails, call records, or payment receipts.

For threats, sexual extortion, child safety issues, stalking, blackmail, or urgent danger, users should seek immediate police help and consult a qualified advocate. Legal Saathi can help organize facts and documents, but it cannot guarantee filing results or replace professional legal advice.
