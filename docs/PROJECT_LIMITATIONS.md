# Legal Saathi limitations and evidence

Legal Saathi provides general Indian legal information and preparation support in English, Hindi, and Hinglish. It is not legal representation, and it does not replace a qualified advocate, official authority, or emergency service.

## What Legal Saathi does not do

- It does not file a case, submit a complaint, send a notice, make a payment, contact an authority, publish content, or represent you
- It does not guarantee a legal outcome, current-law accuracy, court acceptance, or a deadline
- It does not ask you to share passwords, payment credentials, government identity numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information

## Demo and test-data boundary

Use fictional, non-sensitive examples when you try the public demo. Do not upload real case files, identity documents, banking information, confidential government material, or another person’s private legal information.

## Feature availability

Sign-in, AI responses, Web citations, document uploads, saved cases, memory, plans, and payments depend on the deployed configuration and account state. A disabled or unavailable feature must show a clear reason instead of presenting a simulated result.

## Evidence boundary

Local tests prove source behavior only. Hosted claims require separate checks on the exact deployed release with disposable accounts and synthetic data. Screenshots, videos, devlogs, and project descriptions must show only behavior that those checks verify.

## AI assistance disclosure

Development used AI assistance. The project owner reviews product decisions, source integration, testing, and public documentation. Any platform-specific AI-use disclosure should state this accurately.

## When to seek help

For emergencies, imminent harm, or a decision with serious legal consequences, contact the appropriate official emergency service or a qualified Indian advocate. Verify material current-law details through official sources before acting.
